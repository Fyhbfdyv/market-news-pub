Positive Impact: Memory chipmakers like Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung may benefit because AI servers need high-bandwidth memory.

Negative Impact: Nvidia may face valuation pressure if investors keep rotating away from the most crowded AI winner.

Tech-heavy indexes like the Nasdaq may remain volatile because AI leadership is narrowing and expectations are high.
